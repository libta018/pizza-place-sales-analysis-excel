# Pizza Place Sales Analysis (Excel Dashboard & Report)

An Excel dashboard and written report that analyze one year (Jan–Dec 2015) of pizza sales to find sales trends, customer preferences and ways to boost revenue.

![Dashboard 1](images/dashboard-1.png)
![Dashboard 2](images/dashboard-2.png)

## Business problem
A pizza restaurant saw its sales decline. Management wanted to understand customer behavior, purchase trends and product performance to find the root causes and recommend data-driven fixes.

**Goals**
- Identify the root causes of the sales decline.
- Find top and low-performing products.
- Recommend actions to raise sales (target: +10% in 6 months).

## Dataset
Pizza sales data for 2015 from the restaurant: orders, order details, pizzas and pizza types. [Add dataset source / link]

## Tools used
- **Microsoft Excel**: Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP, filters, charts, slicers/timeline
- Data cleaning, merging multiple tables, KPI cards and an interactive dashboard

## Process
1. **Data cleaning:** removed duplicates, handled missing values, fixed formats.
2. **Data merging:** joined the orders, order details, pizzas and pizza types tables with VLOOKUP.
3. **Analysis:** revenue and sales trends, peak hours, category and size mix, best and worst sellers, seasonality.
4. **Visualization:** built an interactive Excel dashboard with KPI cards and charts.
5. **Recommendations:** wrote a report with actions for menu, pricing, promotion and operations.

## Key insights
- **Peak hours:** 12–1 PM and 4–7 PM are the busiest times.
- **Category:** Classic is the top category by pizzas sold (14,888) and by share of sales (26.91%), followed by Supreme (25.46%), Chicken (23.96%) and Veggie (23.68%).
- **Size:** Large (L) pizzas contribute the most to sales (45.89%), then Medium (30.49%) and Small (21.77%); XL and XXL are small.
- **Top 5 by revenue:** The Thai Chicken Pizza ($43,332), The Barbecue Chicken Pizza ($41,683), The California Chicken Pizza ($40,167), The Classic Deluxe Pizza ($37,632), The Spicy Italian Pizza ($34,164).
- **Weakest seller:** The Brie Carre Pizza has the lowest revenue ($11,352) and the lowest quantity (490).
- **Seasonality:** Spring and Summer sales are highest; Fall is the lowest.
- **Customers:** about 60 customers per day on average.

## Recommendations
- **Staffing and inventory:** plan for the 12–1 PM and 4–7 PM peaks.
- **Menu:** review or remove consistently low sellers (for example Brie Carre) and test limited-edition seasonal pizzas.
- **Pricing and promotions:** run Fall discounts, weekday offers and combo deals; improve targeted ads and email campaigns to convert social media engagement.
- **Customer experience:** collect feedback through reviews and surveys.
